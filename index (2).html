Tips

  • Wear wired headphones. If you use speakers, the earlier parts get recorded into the new take.
  • Run Calibrate once on each device and headphone setup (Bluetooth adds a lot of delay).
  • Record the bass or guide part first, then the melody, then harmonies, then percussion.
  • Keep the same framing and lighting for every part.
  • Export renders frame by frame, usually faster than real time. Keep the tab open until it finishes.
  • Shortcuts: R to record or stop, Space to play or stop.
